LEFTOVERUPRISING

The food has come alive and it wants out. Hold the kitchen together: solo, or with up to 8 cooks.

How to play
  • Food marches from the doors to the back door. Every one that escapes costs lives.
  • Tap an empty floor tile to build a Prep Station. Everyone starts with the same blank counter.
  • Tap your tower to upgrade it into a tree: 🔥 Heat, 🔪 Chop, ❄️ Cold, 🍯 Sticky or 💰 Service.
  • Gold you spend in a tree builds your mastery in it: cheaper, stronger towers, and the top tier unlocks.
  • Nobody can master everything, and the food adapts: if the team leans on one tree, the next waves bring food that shrugs it off. Split up.
  • Combos need two trees: freeze then Chop to Shatter, syrup then Heat to Caramelize. Service towers only boost other trees.
  • More cooks means more doors, more food and more kinds at once. Solo, the food stays beatable with one strategy.
  • Bosses at waves 10, 20 and 30. Survive wave 30 to win, then keep going in endless.
