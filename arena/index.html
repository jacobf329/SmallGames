BLOCKOUTARENA

Neon block arena. Phone vs phone, first to the score wins.

How to play
  • Left thumb: put it down anywhere on the left and drag to move.
  • Right thumb: drag to aim. Hold FIRE and drag it to aim while shooting.
  • JUMP gets you onto crates; walk up half-crates to the centre platform.
  • Headshots do double damage. Health comes back if you stay out of trouble.
  • Auto-fire (on by default, in the lobby) shoots when an enemy is under your crosshair.
  • Computer: click the view, then WASD, mouse, click to fire, Space, R.
