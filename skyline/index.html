SKYLINEHUSTLE

Buy the block. Paint the town your colour. Build to the clouds.

Scenario
Rivals
How to play
  • Tap a lot to buy land, open a business or manage it. Drag to pan, pinch to zoom.
  • The city is split into 8 colour districts. Own every lot of a colour to complete the set: ground rent on it doubles and you can stack apartments all the way to a Skyline Tower.
  • Apartments bring residents, and residents are customers. Build them evenly across the set.
  • You can open a business on anyone's land, but you'll pay them rent every night. Rivals do the same to you.
  • Set each shop's price and staff. Rich streets tolerate luxury prices; budget streets flee them. If the shop's queue is out of the door, hire. If it's empty, cut staff.
  • Metro stations pay fares (more the more you own) and bring tourists to nearby blocks. Utilities take a cut of every business's bills.
  • Rivals will bid for the lot that completes their set. You can bid for theirs too.
