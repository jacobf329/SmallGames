Win the land auctions. Paint the town your colour. Build to the clouds, decade by decade.

Scenario
Rivals
📶 LAN multiplayer

Play with friends on the same wifi. Up to 4 players; rivals fill empty seats. Speed changes need everyone to agree.

How to play
  • The city sells land at live auctions at 9:00, 13:00 and 17:00. Bid against the rivals in real time (the clock waits). Tap a city lot to petition for it to be auctioned next.
  • You can open a business on any free lot, even land you don't own; you just pay the landowner (or the city) ground rent every night.
  • Build apartments on any land you own. Residents become customers for every shop nearby.
  • The city has 8 colour districts. Own every lot of a colour and its ground rent and apartment rent double.
  • Run each shop: price, staff, opening hours and stock. Food spoils overnight; empty shelves turn customers away.
  • Every person walking the streets is a real citizen with needs, a budget and opinions, heading to a real shop. Tap one to see what they want. Each block's word on the street tells you what's missing.
  • Time moves through the eras, from the 1930s onward: new kinds of business, smarter fit-outs and taller buildings unlock as the years pass.
  • Camera: drag to pan, pinch to zoom, twist to rotate, two-finger drag to tilt. Or use the ⟲ ⟳ ⛰ ⌂ buttons.
